Thank you for installing {{ .Chart.Name }}.

Your release is named {{ .Release.Name }}.
The controller and CRDs are installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Check that the operator reached the Ziti controller:

  kubectl get ztconn
  kubectl get ziti -A

CONNECTED must show True. The second command lists every Ziti resource in one table.

Publish your first app: https://github.com/aliAljaffer/openziti-operator/blob/main/docs/first-app.md
